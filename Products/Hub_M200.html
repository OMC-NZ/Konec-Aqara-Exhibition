Product Info

Description

Aqara Hub M200 is an all-in-one smart hub that connects Matter and Zigbee devices, bringing Aqara and third-party products together across Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings, and Home Assistant.


Its built-in 360° IR blaster with learning capability lets you control TVs, air conditioners, and other IR appliances as part of your smart home. With the powerful Automations 2.0 engine, you can run advanced automations locally—even without internet—for greater privacy and reliability.


Featuring Thread Mesh networking, Wi-Fi 6, PoE support, and a built-in speaker, the M200 delivers a fast, stable, and flexible smart home experience that stays fully under your control.


Key Features

  • Hub for Matter and Aqara Zigbee Devices: The Aqara Hub M200 acts as a Matter Controller, Thread Border Router, Mesh Extender, and Matter Bridge. It connects Aqara Zigbee and Matter devices to Aqara Home and major ecosystems including Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings, and Home Assistant. Advanced Matter Bridging enables even non-Matter Aqara devices to integrate seamlessly while retaining unique Aqara features.
  • Universal IR Remote Control*: Control TVs, air conditioners**, and other IR appliances with the built-in 360° IR blaster. Advanced learning and sync features keep device status accurate in real time***. With Matter-compatible AC control, you can bring IR air conditioners into your smart ecosystem and enhance them with thermostat-like automation when paired with Aqara Climate Sensors.
  • Superior Wired and Wireless Connectivity: Enjoy flexible setup with both wired**** and wireless options. Power and data can be delivered via a single PoE cable for improved reliability, while dual-band Wi-Fi 6 (2.4 GHz & 5 GHz) with WPA3 security ensures fast and stable wireless performance.
  • Simple Yet Powerful Automations: Create smart actions using simple WHEN–THEN logic. Devices like lights, air conditioners, and sensors respond automatically—even offline. The upgraded Automations 2.0 engine***** delivers faster, more advanced, and highly customisable local automation.
  • Built-in Speaker with Custom Audio: A powerful 90dB speaker keeps you informed with clear alerts for doorbells, alarms, and security events. You can also play custom audio messages for a more personalised smart home experience.

Note:
* For accurate status updates when using IR remotes, place the M200 near the air conditioner (wall mounting recommended).
** Only one IR-controlled air conditioner can use the AC Control function at a time.
**** Only one IR-controlled AC unit can sync when using AC Control
***** 5V⎓2A USB power adapter not included.
***** Some automations (e.g. notifications) require a cloud connection.
