Product Info

Description

The Aqara B50 Smart Lock delivers secure, keyless access with built-in Wi-Fi 6, removing the need for a hub. Control your door remotely using the Aqara Home app, Alexa, or Google Home, and unlock it using a PIN code, mechanical key, mobile app, or voice command—even when offline.


When paired with an Aqara Hub, the B50 supports Matter-over Bridge, allowing seamless integration with Apple Home, SmartThings, and more. With up to six months of battery life, a USB-C emergency power port, AES-128 encryption, and an IPX3 water resistant rating, the B50 combines security, reliability, and convenience.


Smart features such as Auto-Lock, Do Not Disturb, and easy rekeying make it a powerful yet simple upgrade for any home.


Key Features

  • Remote Control via Built-in Wi-Fi: Manage your door from anywhere with built-in Wi-Fi 6—no hub required. Lock, unlock, and check the lock status in real time using the Aqara Home app, Alexa, or Google Home over secure 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
  • Versatile Unlocking Options: Unlock your door in the way that suits you best, using a PIN code, mechanical key, Aqara app, or voice assistant. Offline access and anti-peep PINs ensure safe and flexible entry for you and your guests at all times.
  • Matter-over-Bridge Compatibility: Pair the B50 with an Aqara Hub M3, G5 Pro, or M100 to enable Matter-over-Bridge support. Easily connect with Apple Home, Home Assistant, SmartThings, and other Matter-compatible platforms without paying for a premium Matter lock.
  • Up to 6-Month Battery Life with Emergency Power: Enjoy up to six months* of reliable use with standard AA batteries. If power runs low, the USB-C emergency power port allows you to unlock the door quickly and safely.
  • Advanced Security and Durability: Designed for peace of mind, the B50 features AES-128 encryption and an IPX3 water-resistant rating. It operates reliably in extreme temperatures down to –35 °C.
  • Aqara Exclusive Smart Features: Benefit from Aqara-only features such as Timed Auto-Lock, Night Latch Mode, Failed Attempt Lockout, Do Not Disturb, and Away Mode, offering enhanced comfort and security day and night.
  • Smart Automation Integration: Connect the B50 with Aqara Doorbells and other smart devices via the cloud to enable automations, such as turning on lights when the door unlocks or receiving activity alerts. An Aqara hub is required for local automations.
  • Easy Rekey Support: Keep your existing keys and avoid unnecessary replacements. The B50 supports simple rekeying, allowing you to replace the lock cylinder using just one screw for a fast and convenient upgrade.

Note:
* Battery life may vary with usage, Wi-Fi, router and network conditions.
