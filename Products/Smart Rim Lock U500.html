Product Info

Description

Upgrade everyday home access with the Aqara Smart Rim Lock U500.


Designed as a surface-mounted rim lock, it can be fitted to most entrance and interior doors without mortising, making it a practical upgrade for existing doors. It gives households flexible access through Apple Home Key, fingerprint, PIN code, NFC card, app control, voice control and a physical key backup.


Built with Matter over Thread, durable all-metal hardware, IP-rated weather protection and Aqara Home automation, the U500 is a secure and convenient choice for New Zealand homes where reliability, weather resistance and peace of mind matter.


Key Features

  • Tap to Unlock with Apple Home Key: Enjoy quick, secure entry by adding your key to Apple Wallet, then tapping your iPhone or Apple Watch to unlock the door. Access can be shared through the Apple Home app with flexible permissions and no extra app required. It is a simple, modern way for families, flatmates and trusted visitors to get in without juggling keys in the rain or dark.
  • Cross-Platform Compatibility with Matter over Thread: The U500 supports Matter over Thread and the Aliro standard, giving households more flexibility across Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa and SmartThings. This is ideal for Kiwi homes where different people may use different phones, speakers or smart home platforms. Thread also helps reduce power use and creates a stable, self-healing mesh network.
  • Advanced Fingerprint Reader: The 160 × 160 high-resolution fingerprint sensor is designed for reliable day-to-day unlocking, including for older users and fingers that may be wet or dry. With a false acceptance rate below 0.001% (1/100,000), it helps protect against unauthorised access. Fingerprint data is stored locally and encrypted with AES, not sent to the cloud, and the lock can store up to 50 fingerprints.
  • Additional Unlocking Methods: The U500 gives you several secure ways to unlock, so you are not relying on one method. Choose from keypad entry with anti-peep protection, permanent and temporary PIN codes, offline one-time codes, scheduled guest codes, NFC cards, app remote unlock, Siri or Alexa voice control, and a physical key backup for emergencies.
  • Hardware Built for Long-Term Reliability: With full-metal construction, reinforced dual-hook bolts and a robust cylinder, the U500 is built to help resist prying and forced entry. The IP65-rated outer panel and IP54-rated inner panel are well suited to New Zealand conditions, from coastal air and wind-driven rain to dusty garages and busy family entrances. A built-in doorbell adds extra day-to-day convenience.
  • Reliable Power with Less Battery Stress: Powered by 4 AA batteries, the U500 offers up to 7 months of reliable battery life. Low-battery alerts give you time to replace the batteries before they run flat. If emergency power is ever needed, the USB-C port can be powered by a power bank or compatible device, helping ensure your home stays accessible and secure.
  • Personalised Smart Living with Aqara Home: The Aqara Home app unlocks useful modes and automations that make the U500 part of a wider smart home. Gyroscope-based auto-lock can secure the door once it closes, Do Not Disturb mode helps minimise alerts at night, and One Tap Dual Unlock can pair with other Aqara locks to open multiple doors together.
  • Easy Surface-Mounted Upgrade: The U500 uses a front-and-back surface-mounted design with no mortise required, making it a practical upgrade from a traditional rim lock. It is well suited to many entrance and interior doors, helping installers and homeowners add smart access without major door modifications.

Note:
Apple Home Key requires a Thread-enabled Apple home hub or a Thread-enabled iPhone.
To use the lock with a particular Matter ecosystem, a Matter Controller and Thread Border Router for that ecosystem are required.
Access data is stored locally and encrypted using the AES algorithm.
Remote unlock and ecosystem features may require the appropriate Thread-enabled Matter Controller.
Voice control requires a compatible ecosystem and supported voice assistant.
