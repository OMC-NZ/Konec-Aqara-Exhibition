Product Info

Description

The Aqara Presence Touchscreen Switch S100 brings lighting, smart home control and presence sensing together in one sleek wall switch. Its 1.3-inch high-definition touchscreen provides quick access to lighting, scenes and connected devices, while two built-in relay channels allow direct control of two wired lighting circuits. Swipe through controls, launch favourite scenes or use the physical buttons for everyday operation.


Built-in presence detection allows the S100 to respond automatically when someone enters or leaves a room, helping create lighting and automation experiences that feel natural rather than dependent on manual switches. With Matter connectivity over Wi-Fi, support for major smart home ecosystems and advanced customisation through Aqara Home, the S100 can become both a practical light switch and a convenient control point for the wider smart home. It also provides real-time power monitoring and monthly energy-use statistics through Aqara Home.


Available in Horizontal and Vertical configurations, each with 1,2 or 3 channel options, it adapts perfectly to your setup. Smart and versatile for any room.

Key Features

  • Touchscreen Control That Feels Natural: The 1.3-inch HD touchscreen gives everyday smart home controls a dedicated place on the wall. Swipe between devices and scenes, quickly access commonly used functions and reduce the need to reach for your phone every time you want to adjust something. Aqara Home also lets you choose which linked devices and scenes appear on the screen.
  • Presence-Aware Lighting and Automations: Built-in presence detection allows the S100 to react when someone enters or leaves the room. Use it to automatically turn lighting on when a room becomes occupied, switch it off after an absence period, or use presence and absence as triggers for broader Aqara automations. The specified maximum detection distance is approximately 3 metres with a 120° horizontal detection angle under ideal conditions.*
  • Two Wired Light Circuits, One Smart Switch: Two built-in relay channels allow the S100 to control two separate wired lighting circuits. Lights can still be controlled with a familiar physical button press while gaining app control, automation capability and power monitoring. Built-in overload and over-temperature protection add another layer of protection.
  • Up to 8 Smart Home Shortcuts: The S100 supports up to eight wireless switches. Six are virtual touchscreen controls, while the two physical buttons can also operate as wireless automation triggers when configured in Dual or Wireless Mode. Use them for scenes such as Good Morning, Movie Night, Leaving Home or Goodnight rather than limiting the switch to lighting alone.
  • Physical Buttons That Adapt to Your Home: Choose between three operating modes depending on how you want each button to behave. Dual Mode controls the wired light while also triggering automations, Standard Mode behaves like a conventional light switch, and Wireless Mode turns the button into an automation trigger without manually changing the relay state.
  • Matter-Ready for Multi-Platform Homes: The S100 connects to supported Matter ecosystems through Wi-Fi, allowing it to work with platforms such as Apple Home, Google Home and Amazon Alexa when paired with a compatible Matter hub. Multi-admin Matter support also allows the device to be shared with additional compatible ecosystems without resetting it.**
  • Make the Screen Your Own: Through Aqara Home, choose a full-screen or split-screen layout, select the devices and scenes you want displayed and hide controls you rarely use. Themes and device-card customisation help the S100 feel less like a traditional wall switch and more like a purpose-built smart home interface.***
  • Understand Your Energy Use: The S100 provides real-time power consumption monitoring and monthly energy-use statistics through Aqara Home, giving you greater visibility over the lighting circuits connected to the switch.

Note:
* Presence detection performance can vary depending on installation height, angle and surrounding surfaces. Large areas of metal or glass, curtains, fans and air-conditioning airflow may affect detection performance.

** A compatible Matter hub is required when using the S100 within a Matter ecosystem.

*** Some advanced functions, including screen layout, device/scene linking and other configuration options, are available specifically through Aqara Home.

**** The S100 is a hard-wired mains-powered product and must be installed by a qualified professional in accordance with relevant electrical requirements.
