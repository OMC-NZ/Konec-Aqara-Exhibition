Product Info

Description

The Aqara Panel Hub S1 Plus (Desktop) is a premium whole-home smart control panel designed for desktop and tabletop use. It brings compatible Aqara smart home devices together in one central place, with a large 6.9-inch touchscreen and 6 wireless buttons for controlling lighting, scenes, temperature settings, curtains, smart devices and selected Aqara camera feeds.


The Aqara Panel Hub S1 Plus also works as both a Matter Bridge and Aqara Zigbee hub, helping supported Aqara Zigbee devices connect with major smart home ecosystems such as Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings and other Matter-compatible platforms.


Key Features

  • Centralised Smart Home Control: Bring compatible Aqara and Matter devices together on one shared screen. Control lighting, curtains, climate, locks and scenes without moving between multiple phone pages, then group everyday actions into simple one-tap routines. Create up to 10 dashboards with up to 60 device or scene cards on each.
  • Built-in Siri Voice Control: Use Siri directly from the panel for hands-free Apple Home control. Run scenes or adjust compatible lights, locks and other devices without reaching for your phone. A HomePod or HomePod mini configured as the Apple Home hub is required.
  • Easy USB-C Desktop Setup: No wall wiring or electrician is needed. Connect USB-C power and place the panel on a desk, kitchen bench, bedside table or shelf. Its engineered tilt gives a natural viewing angle, while dual-band 2.4/5 GHz Wi-Fi supports flexible placement around the home.
  • Camera Streaming & Doorbell Unlock: View compatible Aqara camera and doorbell feeds directly on the panel. Doorbell video can pop up automatically so you can see who is there, and supported Aqara smart locks can be unlocked from the same screen after visual confirmation.
  • Matter Bridge for a More Connected Home: Use the S1 Plus as an Aqara Zigbee 3.0 hub and Matter Bridge, helping supported Aqara Zigbee devices work across wider smart home ecosystems. Up to 6 wireless switches can also be exposed to Matter platforms for quick scene and device control.
  • Dashboards Built Around Your Household: Create up to 10 dashboards with up to 60 device or scene cards on each. Auto-generated layouts make setup easier, while editable card sizes, custom icons, device names, themes and screensavers let you tailor the panel to the way your household uses it.
  • Wakes as You Approach: The screen can wake automatically as you come near, with an adjustable activation range up to approximately 1.5 metres. Automatic brightness keeps the display comfortable to view, while auto-off when idle reduces unnecessary screen activity.
